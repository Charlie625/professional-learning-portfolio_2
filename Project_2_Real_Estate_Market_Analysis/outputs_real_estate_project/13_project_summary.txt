Main findings:
1. The total price distribution is right-skewed, meaning most listings are in lower and middle price ranges while fewer listings are very expensive.
2. Total price is strongly related to unit price and moderately related to property area.
3. City-level comparison shows clear differences in median unit price across cities.
4. Price segment analysis shows that higher price groups do not only depend on area; location and unit price also influence value.
5. Room type, orientation, building age, and city type provide useful ways to compare housing value patterns.
